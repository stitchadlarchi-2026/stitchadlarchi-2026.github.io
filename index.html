What we do

STITCH is a theme of connection, assembly and transformation. As the inaugural graduate exhibition of Adelaide University, it marks a moment in which two universities, histories and communities have been stitched together to form something new.

Architecture is itself an act of stitching. We bring together old and new, structure and skin, landscape and building, public and private, permanence and change. Through these intersections, ideas are drawn together to create new possibilities.

Stitching as an act is performed by the hand: deliberate and tactile, made through a sequence of individual movements. In the same way, the work presented are the product of our graduates’ hands, ideas and countless acts of making, drawing, testing and reworking.

Together, these individual threads form a collective body of work.

Who we are

We are Adelaide University’s graduating class from across the architecture disciplines. This exhibition brings together Bachelor and Master work from architecture, interior architecture, landscape architecture, and urban and regional planning.

How we use your details

The STITCH committee collects your name, personal email, your answers, degree, headshot, project title and overview, and poster, and your LinkedIn, Instagram, website and where you’re from if you give them. We use them on the exhibition’s wall labels and catalogue if you exhibit, on stitchexhibition.com, and on STITCH’s social media. Your email is only for the committee to contact you, and is never published.

Your files upload to Cloudflare’s storage service, and your details and files are kept on a server in Australia run for STITCH by NCH Consultancy, with copies held by Cloudflare. We remove location and camera data from your images. We delete the submissions themselves after 31 January 2027. To change or remove your entry, email stitch.adl.archi@gmail.com or message us on Instagram.
