What we do

STITCH is a theme of connection, assembly and transformation. As the inaugural graduate exhibition of Adelaide University, it marks a moment in which two universities, histories and communities have been stitched together to form something new.

Architecture is itself an act of stitching. We bring together old and new, structure and skin, landscape and building, public and private, permanence and change. Through these intersections, ideas are drawn together to create new possibilities.

Stitching as an act is performed by the hand: deliberate and tactile, made through a sequence of individual movements. In the same way, the work presented are the product of our graduates’ hands, ideas and countless acts of making, drawing, testing and reworking.

Together, these individual threads form a collective body of work.
